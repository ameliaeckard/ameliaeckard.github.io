Featured Projects

Spatial Audio AR/VR Research

Research on using spatial audio cues to help visually impaired users navigate indoors via Apple Vision Pro. Combines ARKit object detection with directional 3D audio, targeting 85% identification accuracy and 30% faster navigation.

View

Emergency Fund Predictor

Machine learning models predicting whether Americans can cover a $400 emergency expense. Trained on 12,295 Federal Reserve SHED survey respondents — key finding: 56.3% cannot cover it with cash or savings alone.

View

HamsterFace — Facial Expression Detector

Real-time webcam app that detects 7 facial expressions and maps each emotion to a matching hamster image. Built with vanilla JS and face-api.js, with light/dark theme support and adjustable detection settings.

View

SoundQuest — Location-Based Music Discovery

Designed a location-based music discovery app helping college students collect songs tied to the places they first heard them. Led user research including usability testing and interviews, producing an interactive music map as an alternative to algorithmic recommendations.

View

1-3-5 Task Manager

Productivity web app built around the 1-3-5 Rule: one big task, three medium tasks, five small tasks per day. No frameworks — pure HTML, CSS, and JS with local storage persistence and progress animations.
