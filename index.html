About

Who I Am

I am a software engineer and applied AI researcher at UNC Charlotte, where I am completing a B.S. in Computer Science in May 2027 while continuing in the M.S. in Artificial Intelligence Early Entry program. My work sits between software systems and research: I build tools people can actually use, then study the technical choices and tradeoffs underneath them.

Recent work includes a self-hosted collaborative coding environment used across 20+ lab sessions, an Apple Vision Pro accessibility prototype using on-device object tracking and spatial audio, a human-in-the-loop document review system built with Amazon Bedrock and XGBoost, and research infrastructure for screening and classifying hundreds of scientific papers.

I am especially interested in local-first and on-device computing, computer vision, spatial interfaces, developer tools, and AI used for narrow tasks where it adds real value. I like systems that leave important judgment and creative work with people, and I am seeking full-time software engineering and applied AI roles beginning May 2027.
