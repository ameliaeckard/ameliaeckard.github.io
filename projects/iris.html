Iris

Personal Memory & Community System

Electron SQLite + sqlite-vec Ollama whisper.cpp Node.js discord.js v14 MariaDB Google Calendar API

The Problem

Iris is a two-part personal system built and run by a single person. The name comes from the Greek messenger goddess, and the two halves split along the line her role naturally divides into: the part that observes and remembers, and the part that carries messages and keeps order. Iris Aelios is a passive record of my working life, and Iris Phylax is the guardian of the Hera's Garden Discord servers. They share a visual identity and a name, but they do not share a database, a process, or a deployment.

Iris Aelios: Personal Memory

Aelios runs in the background, notices what I do, and turns that into something I can ask questions of later. When I sit down to write a status update, prepare for a research meeting, or simply remember what I was doing three Tuesdays ago, the information otherwise exists only in scattered artifacts: a commit here, a browser tab there, a conversation half remembered. Aelios keeps the record so reconstruction is a query instead of an archaeology project. Everything runs locally, with all model inference going through Ollama against locally hosted weights, and the only outbound calls it makes are to Google Calendar, scoped to my own calendars.

Screen Capture with Change Detection

Takes periodic screenshots and discards near-duplicate frames by sampling a fixed pixel grid, cutting stored frames by 80 to 90% with no meaningful loss of signal.

Vision and OCR Pipeline

A local vision model extracts a description, the active application, and legible text from every written frame, processed through an in-memory FIFO queue so partial results always stay chronological.

Audio Capture and Transcription

Records system audio and microphone input on demand and transcribes locally with whisper.cpp, so meetings and conversations that never touch a screen still enter the record.

Accomplishment Record

Each day, captures and transcripts are distilled into accomplishment, learning, and development entries, grouped into persistent threads so ongoing work reads as one continuous story instead of scattered daily notes.

Hybrid Search

Full-text search through SQLite FTS5 and semantic search through sqlite-vec run in parallel and merge into one result set, so both exact strings and conceptual matches surface.

Local Chat with Memory

A conversational interface backed by hybrid search over captured history, bounded to recent messages plus a handful of retrieved captures so answers stay grounded and specific.

Calendar Layer

Coordinates three Google Calendars, mirrors busy time across them so no calendar shows a false-empty hour, and delivers morning and weekly briefings.

Iris Phylax: Community Guardian

Phylax handles the work that would otherwise require a moderator to be permanently awake across the Hera's Garden Discord servers: verifying new members, catching spam and rule violations, tracking participation, managing support tickets, and reporting what happened to staff. It does not create community, it protects the conditions under which community can happen.

Member Verification

New members confirm their age and accept the rules through a button-driven flow before receiving the Verified role, with returning members automatically matched and restored.

XP and Ranking

Members progress through seven ranks by earning XP from messages and active voice participation, with cooldowns and activity checks that reward sustained presence over flooding.

Auto-Moderation

Detects spam and rule violations in real time, escalates delinquency points, and applies cross-server timeouts so a person removed from one server can't simply reappear in another.

Ticket System

Members open private support channels through a button, and inactive tickets archive automatically after 48 hours so the conversation is preserved rather than lost.

Self-Assignable Roles

Button-driven role selection across pronouns, timezones, and notification preferences, so members control their own presentation without asking staff.

Ace Hub Reporting

Real-time alerts and scheduled digests report community activity to the staff server via webhooks, independent of the bot's own connection state.

Design Principles

Both systems follow the same core rules. Nothing polls in a loop. Every recurring task, from morning briefings to ticket archiving, computes its next fire time explicitly rather than running on a fixed interval from process boot. Capture never blocks on processing, so a slow or offline model never stalls the thing being recorded. Data is written once and read many times, with derived data cached and invalidated explicitly rather than recomputed. And every component is built to degrade rather than fail outright: a vision failure leaves a frame unprocessed and retriable, a transcription failure still leaves the audio file on disk, and no single failure loses data.
